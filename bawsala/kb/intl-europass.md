## Europass CV

Europass is the European Union's free tool for creating a CV and cover letter in a standard format. It is commonly requested for programmes, scholarships and some jobs in Europe.

## Language levels (CEFR)

When applying abroad, describe language skills with the Common European Framework of Reference levels: A1–A2 (basic), B1–B2 (independent), C1–C2 (proficient). For French and English, a recognised test (DELF/DALF, TCF, IELTS, TOEFL, Cambridge) is stronger evidence than a self-declared level.
