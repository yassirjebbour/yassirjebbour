## The four states of a skill

1. **Suggested** — BAWSALA read your own words and proposes a skill, quoting the sentence that suggests it. Nothing enters your passport until you say yes.
2. **Confirmed** — you confirmed the suggestion, or added the skill yourself. It is your claim.
3. **Evidenced** — you attached proof: a link, a file, a project, or a completed "prove it" step of your path.
4. **Verified** — a third party (for example your institution) confirmed that the experience happened. BAWSALA itself never marks a skill as verified, and an AI model never does either.

## What counts as good evidence

- A link to work you did (a page you manage, a dashboard, a portfolio item, code).
- A short write-up with one number: "I tracked 120 products in Excel and cut stock-outs from 9 to 2 per month."
- A certificate from a course, with its date and issuer.
- A completed portfolio project from your 30-day path.

## Les quatre états (français)

Suggérée → confirmée → prouvée → vérifiée. BAWSALA suggère à partir de vos mots, vous confirmez, vous ajoutez une preuve, et seul un tiers (votre établissement) peut vérifier.

## الحالات الأربع (العربية)

مقترحة ← مؤكَّدة ← مُثبتة بدليل ← موثَّقة. بوصلة تقترح انطلاقاً من كلماتك، وأنت تؤكد، ثم تضيف دليلاً، ولا يوثّقها إلا طرف ثالث مثل مؤسستك. الذكاء الاصطناعي لا يوثّق أي مهارة.
