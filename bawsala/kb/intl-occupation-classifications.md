## ISCO-08

The International Standard Classification of Occupations (ISCO-08), maintained by the ILO, groups jobs into a hierarchy of major, sub-major, minor and unit groups. National statistics offices use it (or national adaptations) to compare occupations across countries. BAWSALA plans to tag each role with an ISCO-08 unit group so that labour-market statistics can be linked to roles; this mapping has not been done yet and is listed in the gap register.

## ESCO

ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations) is the European multilingual classification of skills and occupations, linked to ISCO-08. It is useful as a reference vocabulary for skill names in French and English. Website: https://esco.ec.europa.eu.

## How BAWSALA uses them

BAWSALA's own skill catalogue (26 skills, see src/server/domain/catalog.ts) is intentionally small and written for students. Mapping each skill to ESCO concepts is planned so that BAWSALA passports can be read by systems that use ESCO.
