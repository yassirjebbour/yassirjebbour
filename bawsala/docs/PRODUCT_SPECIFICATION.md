# BAWSALA — Product specification (v1 pilot)

## Problem

In Morocco, measured unemployment rises with the diploma (HCP, Q2 2026: 16.7% for advanced diplomas vs 3.8% without
a diploma; 27.2% for 15–24 year-olds — figures to be re-checked on hcp.ma). Young people often have real skills from
family businesses, clubs, informal sales or projects, but cannot name them, prove them, or connect them to roles.

## Target users (pilot)

1. **Primary:** university students and recent graduates in Morocco (18–28), smartphone-first, writing in Darija
   (Arabic or Latin script), French, Arabic, English or Tamazight.
2. **Secondary:** institution career-office staff (aggregated, consent-based view; verification of experiences).
3. **Operators:** the BAWSALA team (opportunities, knowledge base, support, flags).

## Value proposition

"Write what you have done. BAWSALA finds your skills, the roles that fit, what is missing, and a 30-day path to get
there — and helps you prove each step."

## Core journey (implemented)

Discover (landing + no-account try-it) → Register → Onboard (language, situation, first experience) → Analyze (skills
with the sentence that shows them) → Validate (student accepts/rejects; adds proof) → Match (roles ranked by skills
held, with demo labels and sources) → Develop (30-day learn/practise/prove path) → Apply (application assistant,
interview practice) → Progress (dashboard next step, streak, notifications).

## Differentiators

- **Trustworthy Skills Passport:** suggested → confirmed → proven (evidence) → verified (by an institution only).
- **Darija-native:** analysis and retrieval handle Arabizi; interface in five languages.
- **Honest data:** every opportunity shows source/date/status; demo data is labelled; facts carry citations.
- **Works without AI:** a deterministic engine gives real answers when no model is available.

## Activation and success metrics

- **Activation:** a student confirms ≥ 3 skills and chooses a target role within 7 days of sign-up.
- **North Star:** students who completed ≥ 1 *prove/project* step in the last 7 days (admin overview).
- **Funnel (admin):** signed up → onboarded → confirmed a skill → chose a target → saved a path → completed a step.
- **Retention:** weekly active students (any event); week-4 retention of activated students.
- **Quality:** 👍/👎 ratio on assistant answers; share of suggestions accepted vs rejected.

## Retention drivers

The path (daily steps with due dates and reminders), proof that upgrades the passport, the dashboard's single
next step, and the assistant that remembers the student's context.

## Out of scope for v1

Employer accounts and job applications through BAWSALA, payments, mobile native apps, automated opportunity scraping,
verified certificates/QR passports, voice.
